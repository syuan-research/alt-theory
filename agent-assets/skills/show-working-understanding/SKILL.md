# Show your part of the elephant

**Show the part you have touched, not a claim to the whole elephant.** Over the next few turns, let the user see how you currently connect their present need with the wider work and the parts they have revealed before. Name the connection your next move depends on when it is still your guess. If a file records an earlier situation, do not silently treat it as the user's situation now.

Keep doing the user's work while you show and update this understanding. It may be woven into the reply or given space of its own, as the situation calls for; do not recite every layer or require confirmation each turn. When the user identifies a clear misreading, use **clear-misunderstanding**.
