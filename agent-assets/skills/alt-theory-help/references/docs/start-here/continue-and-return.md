# Continue, Change Direction, and Return Later

Real research work is not linear. It pauses, branches, reverses, and picks
up weeks later. Alt Theory conversations are built for that.

## Conversations are durable

Every conversation is saved locally and appears in the conversation list,
grouped by working folder and searchable. Conversations name themselves
after the first exchange; rename them whenever you like. Reopen any
conversation and continue — its context, materials, mode, and
configuration resume with it.

## Changing direction, without losing anything

You have more options than "keep going" or "start over":

- **Stop** — interrupt a response at any time and steer.
- **Edit or revise** an earlier message of yours — the conversation
  continues from that point with your new wording. The first time you do
  this, the app explains what it rewrites.
- **Branch** — split off a related conversation to pursue an alternative,
  keeping the original line intact.
- **BTW** — open a small side conversation that carries your current
  context, for a tangent that should not clutter the main line. If the
  tangent turns out to matter, promote it to a full branch.

Each option preserves something different;
[Working with Responses and Conversation
Controls](../system-guide/responses-and-controls.md) has the comparison
table.

## Long conversations keep working

Long research conversations eventually exceed what a model can hold in
view at once. When that point nears, the app **compacts** the conversation:
earlier turns are summarized so the work can continue. This is visible,
not silent — a divider marks the boundary, and the summary is there to
expand and read. If something load-bearing must not be lost to
summarization, put it in a file or a plan record; files do not compact.
[Conversations and History](../system-guide/conversations-and-history.md)
covers the details.

## Returning after time away

When you reopen a conversation after days or weeks, what resumes is the
conversation itself — history, working folder, knowledge base, mode. Two
honest notes about the world outside the conversation:

- If the working folder was moved or renamed in the meantime, the app says
  so and lets you re-point it, rather than pretending.
- If your files changed since you last worked here, the agent's first move on
  real work is to read the current files — not to trust its memory of
  them.

That is the product's general posture: coming back should be cheap, and
what the conversation says about your project should match the project.
