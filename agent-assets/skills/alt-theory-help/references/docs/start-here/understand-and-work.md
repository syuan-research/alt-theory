# Understand and Work

Every Alt Theory conversation runs in one of two modes. The distinction is
not "chat versus agent" — both modes are agentic. The distinction is how
much of your computer and tool ecosystem you have chosen to open to the
agent.

## Understand

Understand is for careful dialogue with deliberately bounded reach.

- The agent works with the conversation, files you attach, the selected
  knowledge base, and its own knowledge — clearly labelled.
- It does not browse the web, run commands, or roam your file system.
- There is **no live lookup in Understand, by design**. When a claim would
  need verification, the agent says so and gives you its best current
  understanding, labelled by how solid it is — established knowledge,
  plausible but unverified, or genuinely unknown. It will not dress up
  memory as a search result.

Understand is a complete way to use Alt Theory, not a limited preview of
Work. If your use of the product is thinking, examining, and discussing,
you may spend most of your time here.

## Work

Work keeps the same conversational standard and adds practical reach:

- a **working folder** (and optionally more folders) where the agent can
  read and write project files;
- **live search and fetching** — general web, academic search with
  verifiable metadata, and full-page retrieval;
- **document work** — reading Word, PDF, Excel, and PowerPoint files, and
  producing new documents;
- **commands and installs**, always through a visible approval flow.

Work is not a "productivity mode" that stops thinking carefully. It
carries the same standard into action: consequential choices are explained,
provenance is kept, and your original files are never edited in place.

## What asks for permission

In Work mode, some things are within the boundary you have set, and some
things always ask:

- Inside your attached working folders, the agent works without
  interrupting you for each step.
- Reading outside those folders, network access beyond the built-in search
  tools, and installing anything — these surface an **approval request**
  showing what is being asked and what you are granting. Denying is safe;
  the conversation continues.

[Permissions, Approvals, and Agent
Activity](../system-guide/permissions-approvals-agent-activity.md) is the
complete reference.

## Switching modes

You can switch a conversation between Understand and Work at any time. The
switch applies from your next message. The conversation, its reasoning,
your attached materials, and your role and knowledge base choices all
carry across — switching never forks or resets anything.

A natural rhythm emerges: think in Understand until the next useful step
is no longer more conversation, switch to Work to search, verify, or
produce, and come back when practical work exposes a conceptual question.
The [Using the Alt Theory App](../README.md#using-the-alt-theory-app)
section shows this rhythm against real research work.

## How to choose, in one line

Choose Understand when you want the thinking and nothing else moved;
choose Work when the conversation should be able to reach your files and
the live world — and switch whenever the answer changes.
