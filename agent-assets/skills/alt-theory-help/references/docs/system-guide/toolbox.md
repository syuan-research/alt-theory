# Toolbox

The Toolbox is the small menu beside the composer — the front door to the
app's featured actions. It is deliberately short: a handful of entries
that cover the most common reasons to reach for something beyond typing.

## The entries

- **Ask how Alt works, or fix setup** — opens the
  [Helper](helper-and-guidance.md), the fresh-context side conversation for
  questions about the app itself (and guided setup).
- **Align on a plan or decision** — invokes the
  [aligning skill](bundled-skills.md#adaptive-aligning): the agent
  interviews you to build shared understanding before direction-setting
  work.
- **Plan & record** — invokes the
  [plan-record skill](bundled-skills.md#adaptive-plan-record) for work
  worth tracking beyond one conversation.
- **Look something up online** *(Work)* — invokes
  [live search](search-sources-web.md). In Understand mode this entry is
  not a greyed-out button: it is a sentence explaining that Understand
  has no live lookup and that switching to Work is how to search.
- **All skills…** — drops you into the [command palette](commands.md)
  with `/` pre-typed, where everything invocable lives.

## What the Toolbox is not

It is not the skill system — just a curated front door. Every Toolbox
entry has a longhand equivalent (a command, or a plain sentence), and
everything not featured here is one `/` away. If you stop needing the
front door, nothing is lost.
