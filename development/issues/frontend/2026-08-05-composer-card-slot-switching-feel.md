# Composer card slot — "switching feel" visual system (banked, ~1.6)

Owner direction, 2026-08-05. Not scheduled; earliest 1.6.

The strip above the composer is a switchable card slot (see
`development/architecture/information-architecture.md`, Composer section).
Today the switch is functional but visually flat. Banked direction: give
switching a systematic visual identity —

- a visual indicator of the switch on the right edge of the slot;
- a card-area treatment on switch, e.g. a gradient background shifting to a
  deeper grey while the situational (Steer) card is active;
- part of a broader front-end visual pass, not a one-off style tweak.

Prereq: none. Copy/hints already exist (v1.4.1: one-line hints in the tips
slot on each switch direction).
