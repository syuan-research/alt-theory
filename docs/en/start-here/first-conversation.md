# Start Your First Conversation

A new conversation starts from a greeting, two mode cards (Understand and
Work), and the message composer. Pick Understand (careful dialogue,
nothing touches your files) and start typing.

## A first prompt

Alt Theory shows its character on real, unfinished thinking:

> I'm interested in why people keep using parks differently after a
> renovation, but I can't tell if my question is about place attachment,
> habit, or something else. Can you help me figure out what I'm actually
> asking?

A general chatbot hands you a framework. Here the first response more
likely slows down: distinguishes the concepts, asks what you have
observed, marks what it is unsure about.

## What you see in a response

Thinking, tool lines (reads, searches, skill uses), and rendered tables
and diagrams. See
[Working with Responses and Conversation Controls](../system-guide/responses-and-controls.md).
