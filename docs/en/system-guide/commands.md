# Commands

Typing `/` in the composer opens the command palette: the complete list of
what can be invoked by name, self-documenting as you type.

## What lives there

- Every active skill, invocable as `/skill-name`.
- Conversation commands:
  - `/branch` branches this conversation from here
  - `/btw` opens a side conversation carrying current context
  - `/new` starts a new conversation
  - `/compact` compacts this conversation now, at a resting point you
    choose
  - `/helper` asks the Helper a question on the side

The palette filters as you type, and each entry shows what it does.

## Commands versus sentences

Nothing in the palette is the only way to do its thing. "Let's branch
here" or "summarize this conversation" work as plain sentences. The
palette exists for precision, speed, and discovery. Use whichever fits the
moment.
